# Code Of Conduct

Mobile Game Streamer Toolkit should be a practical and respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear, patient, and constructive.
- Respect different experience levels across C++, OBS Studio plugins, mobile capture, and networking.
- Keep feedback focused on technical accuracy, latency, stability, and user experience.
- Credit upstream projects, protocols, drivers, and third-party libraries accurately.
- Avoid sharing private device serials, pairing credentials, tokens, private gameplay footage, or unlicensed binaries in public issues.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Requests to bypass DRM, content protection, device lock/passcode security, or platform terms.
- Spam, repeated off-topic comments, or bad-faith issue reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments and issues that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in blocked participation.
